What is GGBS?

Slag, refined into strength.

Ground Granulated Blast-furnace Slag is a by-product of iron manufacturing. Molten slag is rapidly quenched into glassy granules, then finely ground into a powder with powerful latent hydraulic properties.

Blended with Portland cement, GGBS reacts slowly and steadily to produce denser, less permeable concrete that outperforms ordinary cement in long-term strength, chemical resistance, and durability.

At Mitija Ecobuild, every batch is manufactured to exceed IS 16714:2018 — ensuring consistent fineness, reactivity, and performance.
